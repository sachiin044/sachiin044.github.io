Bio

I am a final-year B.Tech student in Computer Science, with research interests at the intersection of quantitative finance, reinforcement learning, and natural language processing. I am particularly focused on applying machine learning to Indian equity markets — building systems that are robust, interpretable, and grounded in market microstructure.

My current work centres on an end-to-end sentiment analysis pipeline (india_sentiment_v1) for NSE instruments, combining a fine-tuned FinBERT model with careful calibration and signal filtering to reduce false positives in live trading signals. I also work on regime-aware trading agents that integrate HMM-based regime detection, SAC reinforcement learning, and CVaR risk constraints.

I am actively exploring opportunities in data engineering, quantitative research, and ML engineering — roles where rigorous experimentation and production-quality thinking are both valued.
