Bio

I'm a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student at KIIT, Bhubaneswar. My work sits at the intersection of quantitative finance, applied machine learning, and trading systems for Indian equity markets, and I care about building things that are production-grade rather than notebook demos.

My current focus is Sthiti, a production Python/PostgreSQL feature collection pipeline that ingests NSE bhavcopies, XBRL fundamentals, derivatives, news sentiment, and institutional flow data across the Nifty 500 universe, and normalizes it into a queryable feature store for backtesting and live premarket signal computation. Alongside this, I've built an LLM-based news labeling system (news_signal_llm) and a fine-tuned FinBERT sentiment model (india_sentiment_v1) calibrated for high-precision trading signals, and designed a regime-aware trading agent that combines HMM regime detection, SAC reinforcement learning, and CVaR risk constraints.

Outside of this, I'm active in competitive programming and AI/ML research, and I serve as Domain Lead for GenAI & ML at Konnexions Society. My team placed in the top 20 at the Hack-Nation Global AI Hackathon, run with the MIT Sloan AI Club.
